Hi everyone! 👋

This is an open-source, lightweight website template originally created in 2021. It is designed for small business owners and freelancers who want a quick and effective way to establish an online presence.

🌟 Key Features
1. Direct WhatsApp Ordering: Orders and inquiries are routed directly to your WhatsApp number.
2. Simple & Beginner-Friendly: Easy to customize and deploy without complex backend setups.
3. Ideal for Small Businesses: Perfect for handling direct customer communication effortlessly.

Feel free to use, customize, and contribute to this repository!

This template UI/UX design information flow :
1. HeadLine -> For describe what is your business
2. Flow Order -> Explain to customer, how to order your product/services
3. Term and Condt -> Explain to customer, how is your product/services terms and condition (it's important, because you need to clarify and manage customer expectation)
4. Pricing -> Explain how much is your product or services price


For using this template,
1. You can input and replace the data in file 'data.js', like your whatsapp business, and instagram business account
2. You can input your picture, in folder 'public', and replace the folder and give the name same as with current
3. To change browser tab bar logo, you replace the image 'favicon.ico'

and for better SEO, you may replace this point :
  export const metadata = {
    title: "GO CHECK CAR",
    description : "car inspection"
  };
replace 'title' with your business name and summary of your business in 'description' option
